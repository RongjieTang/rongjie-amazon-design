# Amazon Main Image and Detail Page Module Library

Use this reference when planning Amazon image sets, A+ content, or detail-page creative.

## Image Slots

- `main_image`: clean product-first thumbnail image.
- `package_contents`: show what is included without exaggerating quantity.
- `benefit_infographic`: explain one key benefit with short copy and visible evidence.
- `detail_closeup`: annotate material, texture, parts, controls, ports, formula, or finish.
- `lifestyle_scene`: show realistic use context and buyer fit.
- `scale_reference`: show size or fit without misleading perspective.
- `how_to_use`: explain setup, routine, installation, or operation steps.
- `comparison`: compare versions, old/new use cases, package options, or category alternatives.
- `compatibility`: show dimensions, device fit, scenarios, ingredients, or requirements.
- `trust_care`: show care, warranty, support, safety limits, or supplied certifications.
- `a_plus_opening`: brand/product intro for A+.
- `a_plus_specs`: detail table or structured product facts.
- `closing_reminder`: restrained final product promise and recall point.

## Copy Shapes

- `thumbnail_title`: 2-5 words, product identity only.
- `benefit_line`: one clear benefit plus evidence phrase.
- `annotation_labels`: 3-5 labels around product details.
- `contents_list`: exact included items.
- `step_strip`: 3 concise steps.
- `comparison_row`: two or three clear comparison columns.
- `spec_table`: size, material, capacity, compatibility, ingredients, or package facts.
- `care_note`: limits, safety, cleaning, storage, warranty, or support.
- `scenario_caption`: short real-use caption for lifestyle images.

## English Copy Patterns

- "Designed for [specific use]"
- "Includes [exact items]"
- "[Material/detail] for [practical benefit]"
- "Fits [confirmed compatibility]"
- "Easy [setup/use/care] in [number] steps" only when supported.
- "Built for [audience/use case]" when the product evidence supports it.

Avoid:

- "Best", "No.1", "guaranteed", "medical grade", "certified", "FDA approved", "clinically proven", "100% safe", "universal fit", or measured performance claims unless supplied by the user.

## Chinese Planning Notes

- 主图先看缩略图识别度：轮廓、数量、角度、颜色、包装必须清楚。
- 辅图不要都做成大标题海报；要分工明确：卖点、细节、场景、尺寸、用法、对比、信任。
- A+ 页面更像模块化说明书和品牌页的结合，不要只堆口号。
- 未确认的参数、认证、功效、适配型号都要留成待确认项。

## Audit Criteria

- Can the product be recognized quickly in search results?
- Is the main image honest about quantity and included items?
- Does every secondary image answer a distinct shopper question?
- Are visual claims supported by supplied or visible evidence?
- Is visible English copy short enough for mobile viewing?
- Are compliance-sensitive claims removed or marked for confirmation?
- Does the A+ sequence move from identity to proof, details, use, specs, trust, and closing?
